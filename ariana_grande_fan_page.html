Ariana Grande-Butera (born June 26, 1993) is an American singer, songwriter, and actress. She started her career in the Broadway musical 13 (2008) and gained prominence as Cat Valentine in Nickelodeon's Victorious (2010–2013) and Sam & Cat (2013–2014). Her debut album, Yours Truly (2013), reached number one on the Billboard 200. Known for her impressive four-octave vocal range, Grande has released several successful albums, including My Everything (2014), Dangerous Woman (2016), Sweetener (2018) which won a Grammy Award, and Thank U, Next (2019). She has sold over 90 million records, earned two Grammy Awards, and holds thirty-nine Guinness World Records. Grande is also an advocate for various causes and has successful business ventures, including R.E.M. Beauty and a fragrance line.
